# Engineering Notes Editorial Note

Engineering Notes is a personal build log about AI-assisted development, self-built tools, computing environments, and workflows tested in actual use.

Articles aim to combine:

- What was built and why
- Important design choices and technical evidence
- What happened during actual use
- Personal evaluation, including inconvenience, failure, and changed decisions

This site is not a workplace portfolio or institutional research record. Employer-specific projects, internal systems, organizational details, restricted material, and unpublished workplace information are outside its scope.

Source archives, candidate analyses, interview records, redaction decisions, drafts, and review notes are kept outside this public repository. A sanitized public bundle is prepared and previewed locally first; public commit and deployment occur only after one final external-publication approval.
